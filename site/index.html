About

Side-by-side results of an image-to-3D pipeline: TRELLIS.2 / Pixal3D running in ComfyUI, with and without the TRELLIS+ node pack.

  • baseline: the stock ComfyUI template settings.
  • plus: TRELLIS+ mods: mesh repair, QEF remesh with shell cleanup, feature-weighted decimation, isotropic remesh, photo-to-texture projection, texture enhance, plus more sampling steps.
  • quad: adds Instant-Meshes quad retopology (OBJ with real quads).

Models here are web-optimised (decimated, meshopt-compressed, WebP textures ≤ 2048 px); the stats table also lists the full-resolution numbers. Pixal3D is pixel-aligned, so Match input camera plus Overlay lines the model up with the conditioning crop.

Controls: drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers or right-drag to pan, double-tap to frame.
